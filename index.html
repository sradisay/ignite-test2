Weather & Packing

Late October on the East Cape is about as good as Baja gets: hot bright mornings, the warmest water of the year, and evenings you can sit outside in a t-shirt. Here is the live forecast, and what to actually put in the bag.

What the week usually feels like

Daytime
Mid-to-upper 80s °F (30–32 °C) and sunny. Court-side runs hotter — there is some shade at 8 AM and very little by 10.
Nights
Upper 60s to low 70s °F (20–22 °C). Comfortable on the patio; one light layer is plenty.
The water
Around 84 °F (29 °C) — the warmest the Sea of Cortez gets all year. No wetsuit, no hesitating on the way in.
Rain
Unlikely. This is the tail end of the wet season, so a passing shower is possible but a washed-out day is not.
Wind
Calm mornings, a sea breeze most afternoons. The El Norte winds Los Barriles is known for do not usually set in until November.
The sun
UV index 7–8 at midday, even in October. This is the one thing people consistently underestimate.

Recommended excursions

Book independently — arrange through your hosts, a local operator, or directly.

  • ATV & off-road adventure — explore arroyos, hidden beaches and palm oases on your own quads or with a guided tour. Operators such as Sergio’s ATV, Can-Doo and Quadman Baja offer rentals and tours, with delivery possible.
    sergioatv.com
  • Sport fishing — world-class Sea of Cortez action. Half-day runs roughly $350–$595 USD per boat, shared or private; full days cost more. Operators include Baja’s Awesome Sportfishing, Maria Teresa and the local pangas.
    fishingbooker.com chartermariateresa.com
  • Cabo Pulmo snorkel or scuba day trip — the national park reef known as the “Aquarium of the World”. Guided trips from Los Barriles run about 1–1.5 hours by road plus a boat ride, with plenty of marine life and the chance of sea lions. Roughly $150–$400+ USD per person depending on group size and inclusions.
    tripadvisor.com
  • Horseback riding, kayaking or beach time — easy options along the shore or at Casa Afortunada.
  • Relax at the house — beach time, quad rides, or the pool at the residence.

Dinner recommendations

Not included — reserve ahead, especially in season.

  • La Playa Restaurant & Bar — beachfront views, great for a sunset dinner. Seafood and Mexican.
  • Taquería El Viejo — top-rated tacos, with excellent fish and shrimp, and a condiment bar.
  • La Casita or Barriles Grill — solid Mexican, both local favourites.
  • Also worth a look — Bay View, Campestre Triny, or Smokey’s for a change of pace.
